# Novu Revenue Platform (NRP)
## The Origin
Novu was born to solve the "Revenue Leak" in medical clinics. We found that clinics lose 15-20% of income to poor follow-ups and denied claims. 
## The Goal
To create a "Zero-Touch" revenue department. The AI identifies a denied claim, understands the reason via the 'Analyze Denial' function, and automatically triggers a correction or a patient nudge.
## Tech Stack Notes
- Supabase for secure patient data.
- Twilio integration for automated reactivation calls.
