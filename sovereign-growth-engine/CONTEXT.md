# Sovereign Growth Engine (The Hunter)
## The Pivot
Originally built for the legacy Axon project, we realized this engine was too powerful for a simple CRM. We pivoted it to become the growth weapon for Novu.
## The Mission
To autonomously scrape, qualify, and engage high-value clinic owners. It uses GPT-4o to analyze clinic websites and decide if they are a fit for the "Ibrahim Offer."
