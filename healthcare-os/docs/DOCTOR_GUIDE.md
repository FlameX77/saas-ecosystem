# 🩺 ScribeAI: Doctor's Quick-Start Guide

> **Welcome to ScribeAI**. This guide helps you return 4 hours of your clinical day while building your clinic's intelligence. Ready? Let's start.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Start Your Consultation (2 Mins)
1.  **Open the Scribe Dashboard** on your tablet.
2.  Select **"New Consultation"**.
3.  Obtain **patient consent** (bilingual toggle in the top-right corner).
4.  Tap the **Large Ivory Mic** button to start recording.

---

## ⚡ 2. Real-Time Automation
- **Speak Normally**: ScribeAI is fine-tuned on UAE/India regional accents and medical terminology.
- **Watch the Assembly**: On the right panel, observe your SOAP note being structured in real-time.
- **Flag Moments**: Use the **"Flag"** button to mark critical data points (e.g., "Patient reports a fall yesterday").

---

## 📋 3. Review & Approve (1 Min)
1.  Once done, tap **"Stop"**. The AI draft will be ready in ≤4 seconds.
2.  **Review the Pink Highlighting**: These are areas the AI is less confident about or where DHA/HAAD billing codes require more detail.
3.  **Approve**: Once reviewed, tap the **"Doctor Approval"** button. This:
    - **Syncs** the note to your EMR (Nabidh / Shafah).
    - **Ingests** the knowledge to HelpDoc so your staff can answer questions.
    - **Generates** the bilingual discharge summary for your patient.

---

## 🤖 4. Use HelpDoc at Point of Care
- Need to check the latest **DHA Cardiology Protocol**? 
- Swipe left to the **HelpDoc Portal**. 
- Search "Hypertension DHA 2026" — everything is already there, indexed precisely.

---

## 🏥 5. Regulatory Tips
- **Zero Tolerance**: Every note REQUIRES your physical or digital signature. AI cannot finalize notes alone.
- **Audit Ready**: Every change you make to an AI-generated draft is stored with a revision history for billing audits.
