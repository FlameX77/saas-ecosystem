# Healthcare OS (The Clinical Brain)
## The Merger
On 2026-04-05, we merged Scribe AI (live transcription) and Helpdoc (SOP management). This created a single platform where an AI can listen to a doctor (Scribe) and automatically check the clinic's own rules (Helpdoc).
## The Mission
To eliminate the 4 hours of paperwork doctors do every day. It handles SOAP notes, insurance eligibility, and staff training in one tab.
